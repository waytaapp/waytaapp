# Revised copy

Four versions, each for a specific lawful moment. They are not interchangeable —
the whole point is that the message you may send depends on how the person
arrived.

`[SQUARE BRACKETS]` mark things only you can fill in. The `[FEE SCOPE]` block in
particular is finding C-2 and must be resolved before anything ships.

| # | Version | When | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reply to an inbound enquiry | Within 24h of them messaging you | No consent needed — they approached you |
| 2 | Marketing template | To a consented contact | POPIA s69(1)(a) |
| 3 | Consent request | Cold, once only, per person | POPIA s69(2) + Reg 6 Form 4 |
| 4 | Postal letter | Cold, to the property address | See `postal-outreach.md` |

## 1 — Reply to an inbound enquiry

Free-form, inside WhatsApp's 24-hour customer service window. No template, no
approval, no character limit. This is where most of your conversion should
happen.

> Thank you for sending those through, [NAME]. I have read them.
>
> From the papers, you are at this stage: **[STAGE]**. Your sale date is
> **[DATE]**, which gives us **[N]** working days.
>
> **What I can tell you now, at no charge:**
> [TWO OR THREE SPECIFIC OBSERVATIONS FROM THEIR ACTUAL DOCUMENTS — the date of
> the s129 letter, whether the reserve price was set, whether the service was
> proper. Specificity is what earns the fee. Do not send generic text here.]
>
> **What we would do.** We prepare the affordability reconstruction and the
> supporting papers that put your circumstances in front of the court, and we
> instruct [ATTORNEY FIRM] to do the court work. They are an independent firm of
> attorneys; you would engage and pay them directly, and they answer to you.
>
> **What it costs.** On a property in the [BAND] band our consultancy fee is
> **R[X]**, payable upfront or as R[X/2] over two months. [FEE SCOPE — state
> exactly what this covers, and list separately anything billed by the attorney,
> the sheriff or the court.] There is no charge for this assessment, and we do
> not guarantee any outcome — no one honestly can.
>
> If you go ahead, the law gives you **five business days to cancel** and get
> your money back, no reason needed. I would rather you use those days than feel
> rushed.
>
> Would you like me to send the engagement letter, or would you prefer a call
> first?
>
> — [FULL NAME], [ROLE]
> [REGISTERED ENTITY NAME] t/a Consumer Credit Law
> [STREET ADDRESS], Fourways, Johannesburg

Notes on what changed and why:

- The cooling-off right (C-1) is disclosed and framed as reassurance. You have to
  disclose it anyway; volunteering it converts better than burying it.
- The attorney relationship is restructured (L-1, L-2): they engage the firm
  directly, you are paid for consultancy. Adjust to whatever structure your
  attorney signs off on — but the copy must describe the real one.
- "No outcome guaranteed" appears without hedging around it.

## 3 — Consent request under s69(2)

The one cold approach POPIA permits. **Once per person, ever.** It must sell
nothing — the moment it pitches, it stops being a consent request and becomes
the prohibited marketing communication.

Regulation 6 requires substantially the Form 4 content. Confirm the current form
with your attorney before use; this is drafted to its substance, not a
substitute for it.

> **[REGISTERED ENTITY NAME] t/a Consumer Credit Law**
> [STREET ADDRESS], Fourways, Johannesburg · [CIPC REG NO]
>
> **Request for consent to send you information — section 69(2), POPIA**
>
> We are a consumer credit consultancy. We work with homeowners whose properties
> have been set down for sale in execution. We do not buy property and we are not
> acting for any bank.
>
> We obtained your name and contact number from **[EXACT SOURCE — e.g. the notice
> of sale in execution published in the Government Gazette of DD Month YYYY]**.
>
> We would like your permission to send you information about your rights under
> the National Credit Act and Rule 46A of the Uniform Rules of Court, and about
> the services we offer.
>
> We would hold your name, contact number and the fact that a sale has been set
> down against your property. We would use them only to send you that
> information. We would not sell or share your details, and we would not pass
> them to any credit provider.
>
> **You do not have to agree.** If you do not reply, we will not contact you
> again. If you agree, you can withdraw at any time by replying STOP or emailing
> privacy@creditlaws.co.za. You may complain to the Information Regulator at
> complaints.IR@justice.gov.za.
>
> **Reply YES if we may send you this information.**
>
> Whether you reply or not, our free guides on section 129 letters, summonses and
> sale in execution are at creditlaws.co.za — no details needed.

That last line is the one that earns its place. It gives someone who wants help
a route that costs them no personal information, which is both the decent thing
and the thing most likely to bring them back voluntarily.

**Record a `withheld` outcome for non-repliers.** Section 69(2) allows the
approach only where consent has not previously been withheld, and you must not
approach the same person twice.
